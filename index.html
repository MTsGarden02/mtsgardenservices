About MT’s

A family-run business with pride in every job.

MT’s Garden Services & Landscaping is a small, family-run team based around the Cotswolds, providing professional garden maintenance and landscaping services across Cheltenham, Winchcombe and surrounding Gloucestershire areas.

Our team spans three generations — father, son and grandson — alongside a trusted member of our small team. Being a family business is important to us. We believe in turning up when we say we will, working hard, treating every property with respect and leaving every job looking its best.

We specialise in garden maintenance and landscaping, from regular lawn care, hedge cutting and general upkeep through to larger landscaping projects and garden improvements.

What sets us apart is our attention to detail and personal approach. We take genuine pride in our work and aim to provide the kind of reliable, friendly service we would want for our own homes.

We also believe that professional garden services should not have to come with an unreasonable price tag. We keep our pricing fair and competitive, provide honest advice and offer free, no-obligation quotes.
