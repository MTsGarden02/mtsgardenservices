The people behind the work

A family-run team that takes pride in every garden.

At MT’s Garden Services & Landscaping, we’re proud to be a small, friendly team with family at the heart of the business. Three generations now work together, with Martin, his son Mike and grandson Jacob combining years of experience with the next generation of the business. We’re also joined by family friend Glyn, who has become an important part of the team.

Between us, we bring experience, reliability and a genuine passion for leaving every garden looking its best.

MT
Business Owner

Martin Tustin

Martin has over 20 years of gardening and landscaping experience and is the foundation of the business. His knowledge, practical experience and attention to detail have helped build the reputation we pride ourselves on today. As Mike’s dad and Jacob’s grandad, Martin is also proud to see three generations of the family now working together.

MT
Head Gardener

Mike Tustin

With over 20 years of experience, Mike brings extensive knowledge of garden maintenance, landscaping and outdoor work to the team. Working alongside his dad Martin and now his son Jacob, Mike oversees the day-to-day gardening work and makes sure every job is completed to the high standard our customers expect.

GT

Glyn is a close family friend and has been part of the gardening team for around a year. Working alongside Martin and Mike has allowed him to build his experience across a wide range of garden maintenance work. He’s a reliable and valued member of the team who shares our commitment to doing a job properly.

He’s becoming a valuable member of the team and an important part of the future of MT’s Garden Services.
